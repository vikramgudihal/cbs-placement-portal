Institutional Ethics

Equal Opportunity & Code of Conduct

Standardized recruitment guidelines approved by Chetan Business School Academic Council to ensure parity, meritocracy, and recruiter confidence.

The Single Offer Principle
To ensure fair distribution of opportunities across the entire cohort, any student accepting an official campus offer is respectfully restricted from general subsequent drives.
Tier-1 "Dream Job" Exception (≥ 10 LPA)
Students who already hold an initial placement offer are eligible to compete in elite marquee recruitment drives offering remuneration packages of ≥ ₹10,00,000 per annum.
Tamper-Proof Academic Freezing
Academic records, backlogs, and CGPA are vetted against official university marks cards. Frozen profiles cannot be altered during drive selection cycles.
